The Keepers
of Memory

In a mountain village where no birth was ever written down, one family has kept its lineage alive for three hundred years — recited aloud, name by name, each midwinter. We spent a season learning how memory becomes an archive when paper never arrives.

Elena MarchettiLead Field Researcher

Eight weeks · Forty-one interviews · One unbroken line of inheritance.
