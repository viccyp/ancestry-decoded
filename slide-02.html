Executive Summary

A century of inheritance, read in a single afternoon.

Prepared for the
Board of Trustees
Spring, MMXXVI

Within every living person lies an archive older than any library — a record written in DNA, etched into language, and carried in the quiet rituals of family. This report distils three years of fieldwork across forty-one nations into a single, navigable narrative of human origin.

We trace how migration, marriage and memory have braided themselves into the genomes of today's families, and what those threads reveal about belonging in a borderless century.

"To know your ancestry is to read a letter written by people who never imagined you — yet addressed it to you all the same."
