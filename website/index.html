The Forensic Phenotype Report

A study of your facial architecture.

Each report measures the geometry of your face — facial index, nasal form, palpebral spacing, labial proportion, and the structure of cheekbones, brow and jaw — and compares it against documented reference populations held in our comparative craniometric and soft-tissue archive.

The result is a single morphological affinity score and a richly illustrated narrative: where in the world your features most strongly resonate, and a historical portrait drawn from that correspondence.

Questions

Before you commission.

Is this a DNA or genetic ancestry test? +
No. The Forensic Phenotype Report is a study of facial morphology, not genetics. It expresses statistical affinity — how closely your measured features resemble documented population means — not genealogical descent or certitude of origin.
What photographs do I need to provide? +
A clear, well-lit frontal photograph and a side profile, both with a neutral expression and hair away from the face. You can upload them during checkout, or send them later using the secure link in your confirmation email.
How long does a report take? +
The Digital edition is typically delivered within 72 hours. Heritage and Archive editions are prioritised at 48 hours, with printed folios dispatched separately.
How is my data handled? +
Your imagery is used solely to produce your report. You may request deletion of source photographs at any time by contacting the Institute directly. The analysis is intended for personal and educational use only.
Can I order a report as a gift? +
Yes. At checkout you can mark the order as a gift and provide the recipient's details. Photographs can be submitted by the recipient via a private link.
